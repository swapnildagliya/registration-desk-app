# Leylet Raqs — Registration Desk

A single-file, offline-capable lookup for the **Leylet Raqs Festival** registration desk
(Shoonya Dance Centre, Ghent · 29–31 May 2026).

Open `index.html` in any browser. Type a **Shoonya ID, first name, or last name** and you
instantly see everything that person is registered for:

- **Workshop / Package** — Full Package, day packages, Online Lecture Pass, or single workshops (with the exact sessions)
- **Museum Exhibition** QR ticket
- **Historical Ghent Tour** ticket
- **Gala Showcase** ticket

Tap a card to expand full details and any notes (refunds, duplicate orders, things to verify).

_Data as of 29 May 2026 — Zoho Creator, reconciled against Stripe. Ticket counts are de-duplicated._
